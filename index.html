    The story

    Meet Dr. Veda Cross

    Veda built a window into the past, a way to watch history unfold without ever touching it. During its final calibration the core destabilized. A rift tore open, the present began spilling into the past, and Veda was pulled in with it.

    Now she is in Rome, 40 AD, surrounded by objects that should not exist yet. Every anomaly she finds and explains repairs a little more of the timeline, and brings her one step closer to home.

    The Roman Chronicles

    Six chapters. Six of Rome's strangest true stories.

    Every chapter follows a real event, told across six episodes and eighteen scenes.

    Chapter I

    Caligula's War on the Sea

    Rome and the coast of Gaul, 40 AD

    An emperor declares war on Neptune, lines his legions up on the beach, and orders them to gather seashells as the spoils of war.

    Chapter II

    The Sacred Geese Save Rome

    Capitoline Hill, 390 BC

    Gauls scale the cliffs in the dead of night, and the sacred geese of Juno raise the alarm that saves the citadel.

    Chapter III

    Commodus, Emperor in the Arena

    Rome, Colosseum, around 190 AD

    An emperor walks away from ruling to fight as a gladiator, then renames Rome after himself.

    Chapter IV

    The Caudine Forks Humiliation

    Caudium, Samnium, 321 BC

    A Roman army of 40,000 marches into a mountain pass, finds both ends sealed, and is forced to crawl beneath a yoke of spears.

    Chapter V

    The Great Matron Poisoning

    Rome, 331 BC

    Rome's leading men are dying of a mysterious plague, until a slave girl reveals who is really behind it.

    Chapter VI

    The Eruption of Vesuvius

    Bay of Naples, 79 AD

    As the mountain erupts, Pliny the Elder launches the fleet into the ash cloud to rescue the people of the coast.

    How it plays

    Find it. Learn why. Prove it.

    Find it

    Every scene hides several modern objects in plain sight. Numbered clues point the way, and hints are there when you're stuck.

    Learn why

    Each find opens a card with the real history behind it, from Rome's horseback couriers to the very first eyeglasses.

    Prove it

    Timerift questions test what you just learned. Miss one and a rift opens, with one more chance to seal it before the episode ends.

    Made for every screen

    Play on iPhone, or spread out on iPad where every scene fills the display edge to edge.
