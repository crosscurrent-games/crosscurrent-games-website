Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

CrossCurrent Games Inc. ("CrossCurrent Games," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have, in connection with our game Trapped in Time and this website (together, the "Services").

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at developer@crosscurrentgames.com.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little information as possible, and none of it requires an account. You never need to give us your name, email address or any other contact details to play Trapped in Time. Depending on how you use the Services, we collect the following.

  • A random install ID. When you first open the game it creates a random identifier on your device. It is not your name, your email or your Apple ID, and it is not Apple's advertising identifier. We use it to tell one install's play apart from another's in our analytics.
  • Gameplay data. What happens as you play, for example which scenes you start and finish and how long they take, your answers to in-game questions, hints used, coins and Timeshards earned and spent, the screens you visit, tutorial steps, and when a play session starts and ends.
  • Device and app data. Your device model, iOS version, language and region setting, time zone, the version of the game, and app performance information such as loading times.
  • Approximate country. When the game sends analytics to our server, the server looks up the country your IP address belongs to and records only that country. The IP address itself is not stored or logged. Country lookups use IP Geolocation by DB-IP (db-ip.com).
  • Purchase records. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment card details. For each purchase we record which item was bought, its price and currency, Apple's transaction ID, and whether it was a real or test purchase.
  • Ad activity. Whether an ad was requested, shown or completed, where in the game it appeared, and the estimated earnings Google reports for it.
  • Information you send us. If you email us for support, feedback, press or business inquiries, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the game, including delivering your purchases and restoring them
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the game is played, so we can improve its scenes, difficulty, features and prices
  • To find and fix technical problems
  • To show ads that keep the game free to play
  • To respond to support requests, feedback and other inquiries you send us
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

3. Advertising

Trapped in Time shows ads from Google AdMob. These are banner ads on some screens and optional video ads that you can choose to watch for a reward. When an ad is requested, Google receives information such as your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad, which it uses to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We have set every ad request to be treated as child-directed and to receive non-personalized ads only, so ads are not chosen based on your interests or on activity in other apps. The game does not use Apple's advertising identifier and does not ask for permission to track you.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are shown. You can review or change them at any time in the game under Settings, Ad Privacy Choices. The Remove Ads purchase turns off banner ads permanently.

4. Service Providers

We use a small number of service providers to operate the Services.

  • Apple, for distributing the game and processing in-app purchases.
  • Google Cloud, which hosts our analytics server and stores the analytics data described above, in the United States, on our behalf.
  • Google AdMob, for advertising, as described above.
  • GitHub Pages and Google Fonts, which host this website and its fonts. Like any web host, they receive your IP address when you visit, under their own policies. This website does not use cookies or analytics of its own.

These providers receive only the information they need to perform their function and are bound by their own privacy policies and applicable data protection obligations.

5. Children's Privacy

Trapped in Time is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we treat every ad request as child-directed regardless of a player's age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at developer@crosscurrentgames.com and we will promptly delete it.

6. Data Retention

Your game progress is saved on your device and stays there until you delete the app. We keep analytics data for as long as it helps us run and improve the game, and we keep emails for as long as needed to handle your request and for our records. We delete information sooner when the law requires it.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at developer@crosscurrentgames.com and we will respond as required by applicable law, including, where applicable, the EU and UK GDPR and U.S. state privacy laws such as the CCPA and CPRA.

Because our analytics are tied to a random install ID rather than to your name or email, we may not be able to tell which records belong to you. Deleting the app removes the ID from your device, and nothing collected afterwards can be linked to it. You can change your ad choices at any time as described in section 3.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data travels to our server over encrypted connections, and access to stored data is limited to our team. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Users

Our analytics data is processed and stored in the United States. If you use the Services from another country, your information will be transferred there, and we take steps to make sure such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will update the effective date above when we do, and material changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact us.
