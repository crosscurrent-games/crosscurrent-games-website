About the studio

CrossCurrent Games is an independent studio built around a simple belief. History is one of the richest, most underused playgrounds in entertainment, and mobile games are one of the best ways to put it back in people's hands. We are a small, focused team obsessed with craft. Every scene in our first game is directed and approved by hand before it ever reaches a player, using a modern production pipeline that lets a small studio reach the visual polish of a much larger one.

Our name comes from the idea at the center of that game. Two currents, the present and the past, cross into one another, and the discoveries happen where they meet.

Trapped in Time

Trapped in Time is a hidden object adventure through real history. Players explore richly illustrated historical scenes, spot the modern objects that don't belong, and learn the true story behind each one. A time travel story with real stakes ties it together. Dr. Veda Cross has been pulled into ancient Rome by her own failed experiment, and every anomaly found brings her closer to home.

The game launches with the Roman Chronicles, six chapters and 108 scenes drawn from some of Rome's strangest true stories, alongside Veda's Companions, a sanctuary of adoptable animal friends, and live events such as the Great Emu War.

Where it's going

Hidden object games are one of the most durable categories in mobile, and games that teach something real keep players coming back because they feel good about the time they spend. Trapped in Time sits where hidden object puzzles, narrative adventure and companion care meet, anchored by a subject with almost universal appeal and very little competition. History itself.

Rome is the first chapter of a much larger map. Future Chronicles are already planned for Han China, Mughal India, Edo Japan, the American Frontier, Ancient Egypt and Versailles, each built on the same core loop and production pipeline.
